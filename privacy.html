ABC Bubble Pop – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

ABC Bubble Pop is made by Serna Studios.

We don't collect any personal information. The game has no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no accounts, and it never connects to the internet.

Your settings (volume, music, vibration, theme and bedtime mode) are saved only on this device. Uninstalling the game removes them.
